A storage unit packed badly holds less and costs the same. Worse, it turns
every future visit into an excavation. The difference is a handful of decisions
made in the first twenty minutes of loading.

## The four rules that matter

- **Heavy low, light high.** Books, tools and appliances on the floor;
  bedding, lampshades and empty luggage on top. A stack that is heavy at the top
  falls over, and it falls over when nobody is there to see which way.
- **Leave a walkway.** A gap down the middle costs you a strip of floor and buys
  you access to everything. Packing wall to wall means unpacking the front to
  reach the back, every time.
- **Labels face the walkway.** A box labelled on the top is a box you have to
  climb to read. Write on the side, and write what is in it rather than which
  room it came from.
- **Nothing goes in damp.** A rug, a coat or a mattress stored wet will grow
  mould in any unit, [climate controlled or not](/guides/climate-control). Dry
  it first, however long that takes.

## Furniture

Take apart what comes apart — bed frames, table legs, shelving. Tape the screws
into a bag and tape the bag to the underside of the thing they came off, not
into a box of screws from six different pieces of furniture.

Stand sofas and mattresses on their long edge where the ceiling allows. It
roughly halves the floor they take. Cover them with sheets rather than plastic:
plastic traps whatever moisture is already in the fabric against it.

## Boxes

Same-size boxes stack; mixed sizes do not. Fill every box to the top, even if
the last of it is crumpled paper — a half-full box collapses under the one above
it. Do not pack a box heavier than you can carry up a step, because at some
point you will have to.

## What must not go in

Nothing that burns, nothing that spoils, nothing alive. Petrol, propane, paint
thinner, fireworks and pool chemicals are excluded by every lease including
ours. Food attracts pests, and a pest problem in one unit becomes a pest problem
in the row.

## Before you close the door

Walk the aisle you left and check you can reach the back. Take a photo. Then
write down what is in there somewhere that is not inside it.
