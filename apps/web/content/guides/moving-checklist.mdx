Most of what goes wrong on moving day was decided three weeks earlier. This is
the order that works when part of the move is going into storage, which is a
different job from moving straight into a new home: things you will not see for
months get packed differently from things you need on Tuesday.

## Four weeks out

- **Decide what is going to storage and what is going with you.** Do this first.
  Every other decision depends on it, and doing it last is how people end up
  paying for a unit twice the size they need.
- **Measure, then book the unit.** Our [size guide](/storage/size-guide) has a
  real-world comparison for every size. Book for the day before you need it, not
  the day of — an unavailable unit on moving morning has no fix.
- **Order boxes.** More small ones than you think, fewer large ones. A large box
  full of books is a box nobody can lift.
- **Book the van or the movers.** The end of the month and the end of a
  university term are the two times everything is taken.

## Two weeks out

- **Start packing the things you will not miss.** Out-of-season clothing, books,
  decorations, the second set of everything.
- **Photograph anything valuable** before it is wrapped. It takes ten minutes and
  it is the only record you will have if something is damaged.
- **Use up what you cannot move.** Frozen food, opened cleaning products,
  anything flammable. None of it can go in a storage unit.
- **Tell the people who send you post.** Bank, employer, doctor, insurer.

## One week out

- **Pack a box you will open first** and label it as such — kettle, chargers,
  bedding, a towel, tools, toilet paper. It goes in the van last.
- **Confirm the van, the movers, and the lift or loading bay** at both ends.
- **Set aside what must not go on the van**: passports, medication, keys, the
  documents for the move itself.

## The day before

- **Defrost the fridge.** It takes longer than anybody plans for and a fridge
  moved wet arrives smelling of it.
- **Charge everything**, including the drill you will need to take a bed apart.
- **Confirm your unit number and your gate code** so nobody is standing at a
  keypad in the dark.

## Moving day

- **Load the storage things first, deepest.** They come out last, so they go in
  first.
- **Leave a walkway** down the middle of the unit. Everybody regrets skipping it
  and nobody regrets the space it costs.
- **Take a photo of the packed unit** before you close it. In six months you will
  want to know what is in the back without driving there.

## After

Keep the inventory list somewhere that is not a box in the unit. A note on your
phone is enough — the point is that it exists when you need to know whether the
winter coats are in storage or in the loft.
