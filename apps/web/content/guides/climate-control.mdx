Climate control costs more every month, for as long as you rent. It is worth it
for some things and worth nothing for others, and the difference is not about
how much the thing cost.

## What it actually is

A climate-controlled unit is held inside a temperature range, and at most
facilities a humidity range as well. It is not air conditioning: the unit will
be warmer than your house in August and colder in January. What it removes is
the extremes — the 120°F afternoon inside a metal roll-up door, and the swing
between a cold night and a hot morning that pulls moisture out of the air and
condenses it on whatever is coldest in the room.

That condensation is the real enemy. Almost everything blamed on heat is
actually blamed on humidity moving.

## What cares

- **Wood furniture.** Joints loosen, veneer lifts, solid tops cup and split.
- **Anything upholstered**, including mattresses. Fabric holds damp and then
  grows things in it.
- **Electronics.** Condensation inside a case is how a television that worked
  when it went in does not work when it comes out.
- **Photographs, documents and books.** Pages stick together and ink migrates.
- **Vinyl records, instruments, artwork, leather.** All of them move with
  humidity, and none of them move back.
- **Anything with glue in it** — which is most flat-pack furniture, most
  plywood, and the soles of shoes.

## What does not

- Garden tools, car parts, bicycles, ladders
- Plastic bins of clothing that went in clean and dry
- Kitchenware, crockery, most household clutter
- Anything already stored in an uninsulated garage without complaint

If you are storing the contents of a garage, you are probably paying for
something you do not need. If you are storing the contents of a living room,
you probably are not.

## What it will not fix

Climate control does not dry anything out. Something that goes in damp stays
damp, and mould grows in a climate-controlled unit exactly as happily as
anywhere else. It does not stop pests, it does not stop dust, and it is not a
reason to skip covering furniture.

The one thing it reliably buys is that the air in the unit stops swinging. For
things that mind that, nothing else substitutes. For things that do not, it is a
monthly charge for a problem you did not have.
